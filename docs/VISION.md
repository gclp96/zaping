# Executive Summary

Zaping is a cloud-native business management platform designed to help companies operate more efficiently, make better decisions, and scale through technology.

Rather than being just an ERP, Zaping aims to become an intelligent business platform that connects daily operations, business intelligence, automation, and artificial intelligence into a single ecosystem.

The initial market focus is medical supply distributors, leveraging industry-specific requirements such as lot traceability, serial number management, expiration control, and regulatory compliance. However, the platform is designed with a modular architecture that enables future expansion into additional industries without requiring a complete redesign.
